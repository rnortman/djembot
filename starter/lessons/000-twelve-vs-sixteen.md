# Twelve against sixteen

The bembé bell divides each cycle of four beats into twelve pulses, three
per beat. Listen to how its seven strokes sit against the quiet click on
every beat.

The son clave divides the same four beats into sixteen pulses, four per
beat. Its five strokes are fewer, but notice where they fall against the
click.
